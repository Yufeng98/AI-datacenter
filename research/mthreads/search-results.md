### Evidence Notes for Future Waves

- **Do not scrape headline bandwidth off the S5000 product page.** The only bandwidth figures there (3.35 TB/s, 4.0 TB/s) are competitor comparison baselines.
- **The S5000 headline specs are media-only.** 80 GB / 1.6 TB/s / 784 GB/s / 1,000 TFLOPS FP8 appear nowhere on the vendor page. Corroboration across aggregators sharing one origin is not vendor confirmation.
- **Generation numbering is a recurring trap.** Sudi=1, Chunxiao=2, Quyuan=3, PingHu=4, Huagang=5. S5000 (PingHu/PH100) and Huashan (Huagang) are different chips one generation apart.
- **Hot Chips 38 runs 2026-08-23 → 25**, after this wave; no Moore Threads talk content is public.
