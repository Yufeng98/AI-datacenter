## Key Technical Findings Summary

- **Architecture**: LENA (Light Empowered Native Arithmetics) using TFLN (Thin-Film Lithium Niobate on Insulator) photonic integrated circuits
- **Core compute element**: Mach-Zehnder Interferometer (MZI) mesh for optical matrix-vector multiplication; nonlinear processing native to optical domain
- **NPU 1** (first commercial, 2024): World's first commercially shipping all-photonic AI coprocessor; deployed at LRZ Jul 2025
- **NPU 2** (second-gen, announced SC25 Nov 2025): Enhanced nonlinear processing; 30W vs 700–1000W GPU; 30× lower energy, 50× higher performance claims; shipped H1 2026
- **Server form factor**: NPS (Native Processing Server) — 19-inch turnkey server with multiple NPUs, x86 host, Linux; PCIe integration
- **Software API**: C/C++/Python APIs; Q.PAL (Q.ANT Photonic Algorithm Library); TensorFlow/PyTorch/Keras integration cited
- **Manufacturing**: Stuttgart, Germany (in-house production at TRUMPF facilities or nearby)
- **Funding**: €62M Series A (Jul 2025) + Duquesne Family Office second close → total ~$80M USD; co-led by Cherry Ventures, UVC Partners, imec.xpand
- **HPC deployments**: LRZ (Munich, NPU 1 Jul 2025; NPU 2 Mar 2026), FZ Jülich (JSC, collaboration 2025), 2 more HPC centers Q1 2026
- **Target workloads**: Nonlinear AI (physics AI, scientific simulation, advanced computer vision, robotics, sensor fusion) — NOT LLM inference
- **vs Lightmatter**: Q.ANT = all-photonic analog coprocessor (shipping); Lightmatter = photonic interconnect (Passage, shipping) + photonic compute (Envise, announced)
