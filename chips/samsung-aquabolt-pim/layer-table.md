| Compute Engine | *128 FP16 SIMD processors (4 PIM-DRAM dies × 32 processors/die); each processor: 2× FP16 multiplier + 2× FP16 adder + 3 register files; SIMD execution (all 128 same instruction); peak ~1.2 TFLOPS FP16; FP16 only; Samsung 20nm class process* | *arXiv: one **PIM block per DRAM bank (1-to-1 mapping)**, with internal compute units and specialized PIM registers (IRF, SRF). **Hot Chips 38 (2026-08-25) disclosure**: **16 PIM blocks** sit in the DRAM banks with MAC trees running in parallel; ALU supports both FP and INT; adds a **Vector Register File** (1-kbit, max 64 sequential reads); peak **2.4 TOPS SINT4** (SINT8 act./SINT4 wt.), **~1.2 TFLOPs FP8/package**; **"fifteen" precision combinations** total (integer W8A8/W4A4/W8A16/W4A8/W4A16 + more, floating-point W8A8(FP)/W8A16(FP) + more). Address mapping strategies: **Vertical Mapping** (rows interleaved across Channel/Rank/Bank Group/Bank), **Horizontal Mapping** (adjacent sub-matrices in one bank for row-buffer hits), **Reshape Optimization** (column partitioning, near-100% intra-PIM efficiency). ## Status and disclosure notes

- **Aquabolt-XL**: prototype; no commercial production of the HBM2-PIM part confirmed post-2021.
- **LPDDR5X-PIM**: architecture, capacity, bandwidth, and a named-model benchmark disclosed at **Hot Chips 38 (2026-08-25)**, following the FMS 2026 showcase (4–6 Aug 2026). Simulator and datasheet available on request; SDK with reference tooling mentioned. Still **no production date, no availability commitment, no named customers, no confirmed measured-silicon result**. Trade-press claims of "beyond the research stage" (ETNews, 23 Jul 2026) and "full-fledged sales phase" (Seoul Economic Daily, 5 Aug 2026) remain low confidence and unconfirmed by Samsung.
- **Delivered**: Hot Chips 2026 Memory session, Tue 25 Aug 2026, "Samsung LPDDR5X-PIM: World's First LPDDR based Processing in Memory (PIM) Solution for AI Inference," Karam Hwang (Samsung) — content now public, see 2026-09-13 figures above.
- **Forward roadmap (new, 2026-09-13)**: LPDDR6-PIM in development toward a finalized JEDEC LP6-PIM specification.
- **HBM-PIM successor**: still not confirmed either way. Samsung's Hot Chips 38 HBM base-die tutorial (2026-08-23) describes future "processing elements in the base die" for HBM4/HBM5 — architecturally adjacent but not branded as PIM and not stated to continue the Aquabolt-XL line. Absence of an explicit successor statement, not a verified negative.
- **Target market**: broadened at Hot Chips 38 to server, mobile, and client AI inference (previously "primarily on-device/edge" per ETNews and the arXiv paper's mobile-application-processor framing); Samsung continues to market it under an "AI infrastructure" banner. Its place in this survey is as the successor of Samsung's PIM program, not as a confirmed datacenter part.
